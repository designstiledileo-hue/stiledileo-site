Surrey Feature Wall Design

Feature Walls for Surrey Homes

Custom feature walls for Surrey homes, from larger family rooms and fireplace compositions to open-concept layouts with taller walls that need one stronger focal surface.

Start with the wall that already draws your eye: the view from the entry, the fireplace across from the sofa, or the backdrop behind the dining table. In an open-plan room, look at that wall from the adjoining space before deciding how far the finish should extend.

At Stile di Leo, we create feature walls using Venetian plaster and decorative finishes that feel integrated into the architecture rather than added as trend pieces. The result is a wall that anchors the room while still feeling calm, modern, and refined. Explore our custom feature wall finishes or compare Venetian plaster options for your home.

Where Feature Walls Work Best in Surrey Homes

Choose a surface with a clear stopping point, such as an inside corner, the edge of a fireplace surround or the end of built-in cabinetry. A defined edge helps the finish feel intentional when the wall is seen from several rooms.

  • Living room walls in open-plan layouts
  • Fireplace walls that need more architectural presence
  • Double-height family rooms
  • Primary bedroom walls that should feel softer and more custom

Choosing Texture and Sheen

View a finish sample against the wall in daylight and with the lamps you use in the evening. If the wall sits behind a screen or catches strong side light, compare a low-sheen finish and a smoother sample from your usual seat.

Feature Walls Around Fireplaces and Media Units

When a TV and fireplace share a wall, plan their placement, cable access and the edges of the surround together. For the fireplace area, the existing surface and the manufacturer's installation requirements need review before choosing a finish.

For fireplace-led projects, see Fireplace Wall Vancouver. If the room is more about the full plaster finish than the focal wall category alone, review Venetian Plaster Surrey.

Popular Finish Directions

  • Soft stone-inspired plaster for warm contemporary interiors
  • Marble-look movement for formal living rooms and fireplaces
  • Quiet matte textures that suit oak, beige, and off-white palettes
  • Darker statement walls used selectively in lounge-style spaces

Planning and Cost

Surrey feature wall projects vary depending on wall size, ceiling height, finish complexity, and whether the wall includes a fireplace, TV recess, or custom lighting. If you want current pricing guidance, review Venetian Plaster Cost Vancouver.
