From the walls
to what lives beside them.

Stile di Leo began with hand-applied decorative surfaces. The furniture direction follows the same logic: tactile materials, balanced proportions and a restrained architectural feel.

For homeowners, designers and project teams in Metro Vancouver, the goal is a coherent space rather than unrelated finishing touches.
