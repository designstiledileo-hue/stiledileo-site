# STILE DI LEO - PAID ACQUISITION PLAN v1.0

## 1. Paid Channel Role

Paid acquisition should be used as controlled demand capture and proof distribution, not as a broad awareness spend.

Primary paid objective:

- qualified project inquiries for fireplace transformations and feature walls.

Do not optimize for likes, followers, video views, cheap clicks, or broad traffic.

## 2. Meta Campaign Structure

### Campaign 1: Fireplace Transformation

Objective:

- qualified inquiries from homeowners considering a fireplace update.

Audience hypothesis:

- Metro Vancouver homeowners and renovation-aware residents;
- interior-design-aware users;
- people engaging with home renovation, interior design, fireplace, luxury interiors, architecture, and remodeling content.

Geography:

- Vancouver;
- West Vancouver;
- North Vancouver;
- Burnaby;
- Richmond;
- Surrey;
- Langley;
- Coquitlam;
- Delta;
- Abbotsford only if operationally sensible.

Age:

- avoid over-restricting at launch unless lead quality proves otherwise.

Placement:

- Instagram Feed;
- Instagram Stories/Reels;
- Facebook Feed;
- Facebook Marketplace/Stories only if creative remains premium.

Creative formats:

- before/after Reel;
- carousel;
- finished room image;
- detail/texture image.

Primary hooks:

- "A dated fireplace can change the entire room."
- "Dark tile to a lighter architectural finish."
- "Before replacing the whole wall, review the surface first."

Primary text:

Stile di Leo transforms suitable fireplaces and focal walls with hand-applied decorative plaster finishes across Metro Vancouver. Send a photo of your fireplace to discuss what may realistically be possible.

Headline:

Fireplace Transformation

CTA:

- Send Project Photo;
- Request Estimate.

Destination:

- `/fireplace-wall-vancouver.html`
- project case study for retargeting or warm traffic.

Qualification mechanism:

- ask for photo, city, dimensions, desired look, and timeline before quoting.

Success signal:

- qualified inquiry with project photo;
- estimate opportunity;
- site visit;
- project won.

## 6. Budget Scenarios

### Lean

Use when cash discipline matters most.

- Google Search: small controlled test only on fireplace terms.
- Meta: small retargeting/proof test if pixel/audience setup is ready.
- Creative/testing: organic repurposing only.
- Optional tools: none.

Stop-loss:

- pause any ad group producing clicks without photo inquiries or qualified contact signals.

### Controlled

Best starting mode.

- Google Search: fireplace + Venetian plaster exact/phrase match.
- Meta: fireplace proof campaign and feature-wall retargeting/proof campaign.
- Creative/testing: 2-4 strong existing assets reused.
- Optional tools: none unless tracking becomes difficult.

Stop-loss:

- pause weak creative after enough spend/clicks to judge lead quality;
- do not scale until at least one qualified inquiry source is confirmed.

### Aggressive

Use only after lead handling and quality tracking are working.

- Google Search: fireplace, Venetian plaster, Marmorino, feature wall.
- Meta: multiple creative tests plus retargeting.
- Creative/testing: more edited before/after Reels and project proof variations.
- Optional tools: lightweight call/lead tracking only if needed.

Stop-loss:

- pause channel if leads are unqualified, no photos arrive, or estimates do not progress.

## 7. Paid Channel Decision Rules

KEEP:

- produces qualified project photos;
- creates estimate opportunities;
- generates B2B conversations;
- supports won projects.

INCREASE:

- lead quality is strong;
- estimate opportunities are real;
- response time and follow-up are under control;
- project value justifies spend.

MODIFY:

- clicks arrive but no photos;
- leads ask for unsupported services;
- creative attracts low-budget inquiries;
- ads produce traffic but no contact.

PAUSE:

- no qualified leads after reasonable testing;
- wrong service demand;
- high owner time with weak fit;
- channel distracts from paid work or hot leads.
