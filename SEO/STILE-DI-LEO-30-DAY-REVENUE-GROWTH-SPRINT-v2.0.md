# STILE DI LEO - 30-DAY REVENUE GROWTH SPRINT v2.0

## 1. Executive Objective

The next 30 days should focus on qualified revenue, not general marketing activity.

Primary commercial sequence:

Fireplace transformations -> feature walls -> Venetian plaster / Marmorino -> designers / builders -> project proof -> leads.

The strongest near-term wedge is fireplace transformation because the buyer problem is easy to see, the project scope is understandable from a photo, and the existing Brookswood and West Vancouver case studies provide real visual proof.

This sprint does not require new speculative pages, broad SEO rewrites, daily social posting, or automated outreach. Owner time should stay within 5-7 marketing hours per week.

## 4. Fireplace Growth Plan

### Homeowner Positioning

Core message:

Outdated brick, tile, or heavy fireplace surfaces can often become calmer architectural features through careful preparation and hand-applied decorative plaster, when the existing structure is technically suitable.

Use this when speaking to homeowners:

- "Send a photo of the fireplace and we can tell you what may realistically be possible."
- "The goal is not to cover every surface blindly; the existing substrate, fireplace system, clearances, and condition matter."
- "The finished surface can feel more integrated with the room instead of looking like a dated fireplace insert or tile surround."

Avoid:

- promising no demolition;
- promising every surface can be resurfaced;
- making heat-resistance claims beyond verified system suitability;
- discount-first language.

### Designer Positioning

Core message:

Stile di Leo helps designers turn fireplace surrounds and focal walls into custom plaster surfaces that support the interior concept without looking like a standard trade finish.

Proof to send:

- Brookswood fireplace transformation;
- West Vancouver Marmorino fireplace;
- sculpted architectural feature only when the concept requires expressive custom surface work.

Ideal CTA:

"Happy to send a few completed fireplace examples or review photos if a project needs a seamless plaster finish."

### Builder Positioning

Core message:

Stile di Leo can be a specialty finish partner for fireplace walls, Marmorino, Venetian plaster, and feature surfaces where standard paint or tile does not fit the design intent.

Proof to send:

- finished wide-room views;
- close-up texture details;
- before/after transformation;
- simple explanation of surface preparation requirements.

Ideal CTA:

"If you have a project that needs a seamless plaster finish, I can review photos and substrate details before anyone commits to a finish direction."

### Fireplace-Company Positioning

Core message:

Stile di Leo can support suitable fireplace projects after installation or renovation where the client wants a refined surround or full-height feature surface rather than tile, stone slab, or paint.

Ideal CTA:

"Happy to send a few completed fireplace examples and discuss what types of substrates are usually worth reviewing."

### Minimum Proof Package

If the client does not allow full publication:

- one anonymous close-up texture image;

- no address;
- no client name;
- no room-identifying details;
- internal sales notes only;
- private portfolio use only if explicitly permitted.

Do not publish before photos, full room photos, or identifying details without permission.

## 14. Lead Conversion System

### Step 1: Request Project Information

Ask for:

- project photo;
- approximate dimensions;
- city;
- desired look or reference image;
- preferred timeline;
- whether the surface is a fireplace, feature wall, full wall, or other custom area.

### Step 2: Classify Lead

| Qualification | Meaning | Next Step |
|---|---|---|
| A - strong fit | Clear project, local, realistic scope, premium finish interest | Give rough planning range or book site visit. |
| B - possible fit | Good intent but missing photos/details or unclear suitability | Ask for missing information. |
| C - weak fit | Budget/scope unclear, technical fit uncertain, or low urgency | Educate briefly and keep light. |
| D - outside scope | DIY, product-only, exterior stucco repair, unsupported shower/countertop work | Decline politely or refer elsewhere if possible. |

### Response Templates

A - strong fit:

Thanks for sending the photos. This looks like a strong fit for a fireplace/feature-wall finish review. The next useful step is confirming approximate dimensions, surface condition, and the finish direction you like. From there I can give a realistic planning range or recommend a site visit.

B - possible fit:

Thanks, this may be possible, but I need a little more information before giving useful guidance. Could you send one wider photo of the full wall, approximate dimensions, your city, and one or two reference images for the look you want?

C - weak fit:

Thanks for reaching out. Based on what I can see, I would want to understand the surface and budget expectations before recommending a decorative plaster finish. These finishes are best for focal surfaces where the result is meant to be a visible design feature.

D - outside scope:

Thank you for checking. This is outside the kind of work Stile di Leo is currently focused on. We are mainly taking on fireplace transformations, feature walls, Venetian plaster, Marmorino, and decorative interior finishes where a custom applied surface is the right fit.
