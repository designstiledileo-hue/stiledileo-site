# STILE DI LEO - 30-DAY LEAD GENERATION ENGINE v1.0

## 1. Executive Objective

This 30-day engine is built to generate qualified project opportunities, not traffic for its own sake.

The website is now the proof and conversion infrastructure. The main bottleneck is distribution:

CONTENT / ADS / GOOGLE / B2B -> SERVICE PAGE -> PROJECT PROOF -> EASY CONTACT -> ESTIMATE -> WON PROJECT -> NEW PROJECT PROOF -> REPEAT.

Primary commercial sequence:

Fireplaces -> Feature Walls -> Venetian / Marmorino -> Designers / Builders -> Project Proof -> Qualified Leads.

## 4. Fireplace Acquisition Offer

Primary offer:

Send a photo of your existing fireplace and Stile di Leo will review what finish direction may realistically be possible.

Homeowner promise:

- clear next-step guidance;
- realistic project fit review;
- no pressure;
- no exact quote from a single photo;
- no universal claim that every fireplace can be resurfaced.

### Five Homeowner Messaging Angles

| Angle | Use When | Message | Proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| Outdated fireplace transformation | Brick/tile looks dated | The fireplace can often become a calmer focal point when the existing structure is suitable. | Brookswood |
| Dark tile to lighter surface | Heavy/dark fireplace dominates room | A lighter Marmorino-style finish can soften the fireplace and room atmosphere. | West Vancouver |
| Architectural focal point | Homeowner wants more than paint | Decorative plaster can make the fireplace feel integrated with the room. | Both fireplace case studies |
| Alternative to ordinary tile | Tile replacement feels too segmented | A seamless plaster finish may suit projects where tile looks too busy. | West Vancouver detail |
| Photo-first estimate | User does not know where to start | A photo helps assess surface, scale, and finish direction before pricing. | Fireplace owner page |

### Designer Offer

Stile di Leo provides specialist fireplace and focal-wall finishes when a design calls for a calmer plaster surface rather than paint, tile, or panels.

CTA:

"Happy to send completed fireplace examples or review a wall/fireplace surface before finish direction is finalized."

### Builder Offer

Stile di Leo supports custom residential projects where a visible fireplace or focal wall needs specialty plaster execution.

CTA:

"I can review photos and substrate details before anyone commits to a finish direction."

### Fireplace-Company Offer

Stile di Leo can support suitable fireplace clients who need a refined surround or full-height finish after fireplace work is installed or renovated.

CTA:

"Happy to define what types of projects are usually suitable and send examples."

## 18. Lead Response System

Response speed matters more than perfect wording.

First response should ask for:

- photo;
- approximate dimensions;
- city;
- desired look/reference;
- timeline.

Lead classes:

| Class | Meaning | Response Path |
|---|---|---|
| A - strong fit / high intent | Local, clear project, photos available, premium finish interest | Give next steps, planning range if appropriate, or book site visit. |
| B - strong fit / early research | Good project but early timeline or missing details | Educate briefly and ask for missing information. |
| C - uncertain fit | Technical suitability, budget, or scope unclear | Ask for photos/details; avoid overcommitting. |
| D - outside current services | DIY/product/exterior stucco/unsupported wet areas | Decline politely. |

Use text conversation when:

- the lead has photos and needs quick guidance.

Use phone call when:

- the lead is high intent, local, and project scope needs discussion.

Use remote preliminary range when:

- photos, dimensions, and scope are clear enough.

Use site visit when:

- surface condition, fireplace/system details, or scope cannot be assessed remotely.

Use sample meeting when:

- the project is qualified and finish direction matters.

Decline when:

- outside current services, unrealistic budget, unsupported application, or poor fit.

## 19. Response Templates

A - strong fit:

Thanks for sending the photos. This looks like a strong fit for a fireplace/feature-wall finish review. The next useful step is confirming approximate dimensions, surface condition, and the finish direction you like. From there I can give a realistic planning range or recommend a site visit.

B - early research:

Thanks, this could be worth exploring. The most helpful next details are one wider photo of the full wall, approximate dimensions, city, timeline, and one or two reference images for the look you want.

C - uncertain fit:

Thanks for reaching out. I would want to understand the surface and project expectations before recommending a decorative plaster finish. Could you send a wider photo, dimensions, city, and what you are hoping to change?

D - outside scope:

Thank you for checking. This is outside the kind of work Stile di Leo is currently focused on. We are mainly taking on fireplace transformations, feature walls, Venetian plaster, Marmorino, and decorative interior finishes where a custom applied surface is the right fit.

## 24. Stop / Continue Rules

Keep a channel if:

- it produces project photos;
- it creates qualified estimate opportunities;
- it gets B2B replies;
- it contributes to won project value.

Increase if:

- lead quality is high;
- follow-up capacity is under control;
- project value supports spend or time.

Modify if:

- clicks arrive but no photos;
- inquiries are unclear;
- users ask for unsupported services;
- creative attracts low-budget projects.

Pause if:

- wrong service demand;
- poor lead quality;
- no estimate opportunities;
- owner time becomes too high;
- no proof of qualified pipeline after a fair test.
