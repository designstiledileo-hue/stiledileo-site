## 7. Case Study Architecture

Canonical proof-led case study structure:

1. BEFORE / AFTER HERO
   - immediate visual transformation
2. PROJECT SNAPSHOT
   - location, service, original surface, finish, duration
3. THE CLIENT'S PROBLEM
   - short factual description
4. BEFORE
   - what existed and why it needed transformation
5. PREPARATION
   - mesh, base coat, primer, leveling, substrate preparation only if verified
6. FINISH
   - San Marco Marmorino Antico or other verified finish
7. PROCESS PROOF
   - real process photography
8. AFTER
   - large final photography
9. DETAIL
   - texture, movement, finish close-ups
10. RESULT
   - factual transformation summary
11. CTA
   - send a photo, approximate dimensions, and city

## 16. Authenticity Policy

For project-proof assets, prohibited:

- AI regeneration of project photos
- generative fill changing architecture
- removing fireplace projections
- adding/removing mantel geometry
- altering arch/opening geometry
- replacing surface geometry
- fabricating finished texture
- substituting another project
- fake before/after

Allowed:

- crop
- exposure correction
- white balance
- mild perspective correction
- compression
- resizing
- privacy redaction where needed

The physical project must remain truthful.
