## 2. Venetian Plaster Ownership Findings

/venetian-plaster-vancouver.html was already the correct canonical owner for "venetian plaster vancouver" because it has the direct H1, canonical URL, broad application coverage, service copy, and internal links to narrower supporting pages.

Opportunity found: the title and intro could more clearly identify the page as the main Vancouver service page rather than another topical Venetian plaster page. Supporting pages already retained pricing, comparison, condo, bathroom ideas, and geographic intent.

## 3. Fireplace Ownership Findings

/fireplace-wall-vancouver.html was already the correct canonical owner for broad fireplace work, but the pre-change title and H1 emphasized "fireplace wall design" more than "fireplace transformation" commercial intent.

Opportunity found: update title, meta, H1, intro, and CTA to better match "plaster fireplace vancouver" and "fireplace transformations Vancouver" while preserving support for Venetian plaster, Marmorino, brick/tile review, surround, TV wall, and marble-look directions.

## 4. Cannibalization / Overlap Findings

No destructive duplicate page was found. The observed overlap is query-sharing among legitimate supporting pages:

- Cost pages belong to pricing and budgeting intent.
- Comparison pages belong to decision-stage intent.
- Fireplace surround belongs to surround-only material detail.
- Fireplace ideas belongs to inspiration intent.
- Condo, bathroom, and local pages belong to application or geographic modifiers.

The recommended strategy is differentiation through titles, short copy, and contextual internal links, not redirects, merges, or deletions.
