- 10 minutes

Questions:

1. How many new inquiries?
2. How many were qualified?
3. Where did they come from?
4. How many quotes?
5. What is total quoted value?
6. What was won?
7. Which B2B prospects replied?
8. Which relationships look promising?
9. Were reviews requested/received?
10. What single activity deserves more time next week?

## 16. KPI Definitions

Qualified lead:

- service fit
- location fit
- real project
- sufficient intent/context to continue

Quote:

- project price/range or formal estimate sent.

Won project:

- customer accepted the project/quote.

B2B reply:

- real human response from prospect.

B2B opportunity:

- a specific project, referral path, collaboration discussion, or future-fit relationship emerges.

Review received:

- new legitimate public customer review received.

## 17. Revenue Attribution Rules

Use evidence hierarchy:

1. explicit campaign/UTM evidence;
2. direct platform evidence;
3. landing page + GA4/source evidence;
4. customer statement;
5. `unknown`.

Use:

- DIRECTLY ATTRIBUTED
- LIKELY ATTRIBUTED
- UNKNOWN

Do not manufacture precision.
