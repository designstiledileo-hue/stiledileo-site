## Purpose and truthful positioning

A commercial/application page for designers, architects, builders, millwork teams and retail operators. The page intentionally does not claim that the pictured retail interior is a completed Stile di Leo commission. Copy describes the finish application and material direction; it includes no invented client, address, construction history, product system or performance specification. This internal provenance distinction is not rendered as customer-facing disclaimer text.

Existing dark background, gold accents, Cormorant Garamond headings, Montserrat body copy and contact architecture are retained.
