## 2. Offer and business boundaries

Homeowner offer: send a photo of the fireplace or wall you want to change, its location and approximate dimensions. Stile di Leo reviews the surface and discusses a suitable finish and next step. No exact-price-from-photo or universal no-demolition promise.

Partner offer: specialist hand-applied decorative finishes for selected fireplace surrounds, feature walls and interior surfaces. Review the finish scope alongside existing construction, adjacent work and the partner's schedule. Do not imply that Stile di Leo performs gas, appliance, structural or complete renovation work.

Sell verified fireplace transformations, feature walls, Venetian plaster, Marmorino and supported decorative interior work. Microcement remains limited to accepted dry interior applications. No wet-room, shower, Tadelakt, countertop or exterior stucco-repair marketing. Sculptural examples do not establish a sculpted-stone fireplace service.

Mediterranean-inspired remains an appearance hypothesis. Use the existing customer-language pack for matched message testing; do not rebrand every project or claim Canadian search demand has been measured.

## 6. Weekly customer and sales scoreboard

Use the existing lead log as the source of truth, with one ID per actual inquiry. Multiple messages from one person about one project are not multiple leads. Keep actual logs private.

| Measure | Definition | Owner decision |
|---|---|---|
| Actual inquiries | People making project contact, deduplicated | Which channels create conversations? |
| Qualified opportunities | Supported application, workable location and sufficient scope to continue | Which channels bring suitable projects? |
| Consultations/site visits | Actually held or booked; distinguish the two | Are next steps being agreed? |
| Estimates sent | Actually delivered with a defined scope | Is the pipeline progressing? |
| Won projects | Accepted projects; separate pending decisions | Which work turns into business? |
| Accepted project value | Value of accepted work, not cash received | Is the channel commercially useful? |
| Loss reasons | Timing, unsupported scope, geography, price, no reply, other | What should targeting or follow-up change? |
| Partner conversations | Actual replies or discussions, separate from intros sent | Are relationships developing? |
| Review requests / reviews received | Separate counts | Is the review process running? |

For paid channels later: cost per qualified inquiry = attributable spend / qualified inquiries from the same source and period. If none are qualified, report spend and zero qualified inquiries rather than a misleading zero cost. Estimate-to-win rate needs enough resolved estimates from a comparable cohort; mark pending outcomes separately.

CTA clicks and form starts are supporting diagnostics. An accepted form submission is an inquiry, not automatically a qualified customer. Existing project-card clicks also use project_photo_click: filter by destination and CTA location/text before interpreting photo-contact intent.
