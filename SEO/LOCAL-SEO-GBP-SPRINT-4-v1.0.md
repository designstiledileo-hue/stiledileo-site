## 9. Review Acquisition System

Ethical workflow:

1. Complete the project.
2. Confirm the customer is satisfied.
3. Finish cleanup and final photography.
4. Send one honest Google review request.
5. Send one polite follow-up if there is no response.
6. Stop.

Do not:

- Buy reviews
- Review-gate
- Offer compensation for positive reviews
- Tell customers what keywords to include
- Pressure customers repeatedly
