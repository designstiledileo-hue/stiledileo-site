## E. Fireplace Validation

### Refined vs Sculptural Sales Presentation

For relevant future fireplace leads, present two directions:

Direction A - Refined:

- Marmorino / Venetian plaster;
- smoother architectural fireplace finish;
- proven within current Stile di Leo fireplace positioning.

Direction B - Sculptural:

- architectural sculpted stone direction;
- concept only for fireplace-adjacent validation until technical gate is complete;
- not represented as a completed fireplace installation.

Track:

- preferred direction;
- reason for preference;
- whether sculptural direction increased perceived value;
- whether the client asked for pricing;
- whether technical questions appeared;
- whether a quote opportunity was created.

### Technical Blockers

Before accepting a real sculpted-stone fireplace installation, document project-specific:

- appliance manufacturer requirements;
- clearances;
- combustible/non-combustible zones;
- substrate;
- attachment method;
- material suitability;
- heat exposure;
- edge transitions;
- weight/load implications.

Professional verification required where applicable.
