Name: SLD_Search_Fireplace_Local_v1.
Network: Google Search only for the initial controlled test; do not automatically enable Display expansion or Search Partners.
Offer: decorative finishing of suitable fireplace walls and surrounds, not gas servicing, appliance sales or structural repairs.
Final URL for both proposed groups: https://stiledileo.com/fireplace-wall-vancouver.html
Primary contact destination on the website: existing photo-review form.

Geography: choose the smallest owner-confirmed service area that can be worked economically. Candidate locations are Vancouver, West Vancouver, North Vancouver, Burnaby, Richmond, Surrey, Langley, Coquitlam and Delta; inclusion is not automatic. Do not add distant cities to increase volume.

Location option: evaluate Presence (people in or regularly in selected locations) for this local service. It reduces interest-only geographic reach but is not perfectly accurate. Check actual geographic results. [Google location documentation](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722038).

Language: English initially, matching the available landing page. Schedule and call availability: owner decision; show any call asset only when calls can be handled. Do not advertise 24/7 service.

Start with group A if approved. Group B is an optional second intent test after search terms and inquiry quality are understood. Do not split a small, unknown budget across four service campaigns. Venetian plaster, feature walls and Marmorino standalone campaigns are later candidates, not this launch.

## 6. Attribution and conversions

Use account auto-tagging if available and approved; do not disable an existing working setup. Human-readable campaign parameters can additionally use:

utm_source=google
utm_medium=cpc
utm_campaign=fireplace_search_local_v1
utm_content=plaster_a (or transformation_b)

Add these through the appropriate final URL suffix after checking the account's tracking template. Do not duplicate existing parameters or place personal information in URLs.

The current site passes UTMs to the inquiry and tracks accepted form submission separately from CTA clicks. Confirm actual form receipt and campaign attribution in the live environment before spend. Do not assume the current code implements GCLID storage or offline conversion imports; those were not implemented in this sprint.

Account review before launch:

- Inspect the existing conversion actions to avoid duplicate primary goals.
- Accepted form submission can be an inquiry goal after receipt is verified.
- Estimate clicks, photo-button clicks, gallery navigation and phone-link clicks remain secondary intent signals.
- A phone-link click is not a connected or qualified call.
- Qualified/won lead imports are a later measurement step requiring explicit configuration; do not fabricate outcomes or send contact fields in GA4 events.

## 7. Budget, bidding and decision rules

No numeric budget, CPC cap or target CPA is assigned. The owner has not decided affordable spend, capacity or acquisition economics. Bidding remains UNASSIGNED pending keyword estimates and actual conversion data. Do not choose an invented target CPA or claim an expected CPL.

Before approval record an affordable total test-spend ceiling, the account's daily budget and notification/review responsibility. Google's average daily budget is not a hard same-day cap: many campaigns can spend up to twice it on an individual day, with monthly limits governed by the platform. [Google budget guidance](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1704443). Check current account-specific rules and budget-change effects at launch.

After launch, inspect queries and geography early and regularly. Stop for broken contact flow, misleading ads, repeated unsupported-service inquiries or the approved spend ceiling. Do not scale from cheap clicks or a few submissions. Evaluate qualified inquiries, estimates and resolved sales by source, leaving pending or small samples inconclusive.
