What Marmorino Is

Marmorino is a decorative plaster applied by hand. Its layered colour and trowel marks give walls a varied appearance rather than a uniform painted colour. Texture and sheen depend on the selected product and application.

Start with the Look You Want

Warm neutrals and soft texture can suit a Mediterranean-inspired interior; a quieter surface can work in a contemporary room. Bring a reference you like, and we can discuss which finish may suit your wall.

Marmorino for Fireplaces

Fireplace walls are one of the strongest places to consider Marmorino. On suitable prepared surfaces, it can help an existing or newly planned fireplace feel more continuous, architectural, and integrated with the room.

Fireplace work depends on the existing construction, substrate condition, clearances and selected finish. Each project needs review before a scope is confirmed. See our fireplace transformations for project and planning information.

Preparation Matters

The final appearance depends heavily on what happens before the decorative finish. Substrate condition, leveling, edges, corners, and surface preparation all affect how clean and refined the finished Marmorino wall will feel.

Stile di Leo reviews the wall, surrounding materials and desired appearance before recommending an approach.

Colour, Texture and Sheen

Consider how much variation you want to see, how pronounced the texture should be and how much light the surface should reflect. The available combinations depend on the product and application.

  • Colour variation: from subtle shading to more visible trowel marks
  • Texture: a smooth appearance or more pronounced surface detail
  • Sheen: selected around daylight, lamps and adjacent materials

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Marmorino?
Marmorino is a decorative plaster applied by hand, with layered colour and a smooth or textured appearance depending on the product and application.

Is Marmorino the same as Venetian plaster?
Marmorino is part of the family of decorative finishes often called Venetian plaster. Compare the specific product, texture and sheen rather than choosing by the name alone.

Can Marmorino be used on fireplace walls?
Yes, when the fireplace surface and project conditions are suitable and properly prepared. The existing construction should be reviewed before confirming scope.

How is Marmorino priced?
Pricing depends on preparation, wall size, detailing, access and finish complexity. Photos and approximate dimensions help us review the scope; a photo alone does not confirm a final price.

Can the colour and sheen be customized?
Colour and sheen options depend on the selected product and application. We consider these alongside your lighting and surrounding materials.
