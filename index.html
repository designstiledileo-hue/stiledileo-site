Why Stile di Leo

The work starts with the surface.

Edges, existing surfaces and adjacent materials determine how we prepare the area. The finish system and the way its layers are applied by hand shape the depth, texture and sheen you see in the room.

Those preparation and application steps are part of the estimate. For commercial projects, we coordinate the specialty finish with the project team’s scope, samples and schedule.

Before You Start

Common questions.

How much does Venetian plaster cost in Vancouver?

Cost depends on preparation, surface area, access and the chosen finish. Share dimensions and a few project details to help us review your scope. Read the Vancouver pricing guide.

Can plaster be applied over an existing fireplace?

It depends on the substrate, its condition and the fireplace manufacturer’s requirements. We review compatibility and clearances before proposing a finish. Explore fireplace transformations.

Do I need to choose a finish before contacting you?

No. Tell us which surface you want to change and what you like. We can discuss materials after reviewing the project.

How are Venetian plaster and Marmorino different?

Venetian plaster describes a family of decorative finishes. Marmorino is a mineral plaster option often chosen for its gently textured, stone-like appearance. Learn more about Marmorino.

Do you work with designers and general contractors?

Yes. Share the finish scope, drawings or specifications and schedule. We can discuss samples, preparation and coordination with your team.

How long will the work take?

Timing depends on surface preparation, area, finish system and site access. We discuss the schedule after reviewing the scope.

Which areas do you serve?

We work across Metro Vancouver, including Vancouver, Burnaby, Richmond, the North Shore, White Rock and Langley. Tell us where the work is planned.

Do I need a photo to request an estimate?

No. You can send your name and an email address or phone number without a photo. If you have an image, it can help us understand the existing surface and what you have in mind.
