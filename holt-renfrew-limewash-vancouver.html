Limewash at Holt Renfrew

A decorative wall finish developed by hand at commercial scale.

Stile di Leo completed specialty decorative finish application within a Holt Renfrew retail environment in Vancouver. The work centred on substantial wall areas, subtle tonal movement and the way the surface reads as part of the architecture.

01 / Context

A surface at architectural scale.

On a broad retail wall, the visual character of a finish has to work at different viewing distances. Movement that appears subtle close up can become more apparent across the room and under directional lighting.

The focus of Stile di Leo's scope was the specialty decorative finish. This work formed one part of a wider construction project and does not imply a direct contract with, or endorsement by, Holt Renfrew.

02 / Application

Controlled variation, applied by hand.

A limewash-style decorative wall finish has an intentionally different appearance from conventional flat paint. Subtle shifts in tone and visible movement create depth without relying on repeated printed patterns.

At commercial scale, the overall composition matters as much as individual brush movement. Adjacent areas need to read as one considered surface.

03 / Material & light

Designed to sit within the interior.

Retail lighting, surrounding materials, fixtures and merchandise change the way a decorative wall finish is perceived. The surface should contribute depth without competing with the elements the space is designed to display.

Preparation and the surrounding construction scopes are coordinated with the responsible trades unless separately agreed.
