Surface & craftsmanship

Marmo Antico in light

From across the room, Marmo Antico reads as a warm, continuous surface. Up close, the mineral depth and tactile grain become more apparent. Light grazing the long wall catches small variations in the hand-applied finish, so its tone changes as you move through the room.
