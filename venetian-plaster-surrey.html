Choosing a Finish in the Room's Light

Side lighting makes texture more noticeable; broad daylight and evening lighting can give the same wall a different appearance. Share photographs in both conditions if possible. We can discuss how much texture and sheen you want, and whether a sample is appropriate before the work is agreed.

Considering a Fireplace Transformation?

An existing brick or tiled surround may be suitable for resurfacing, but the substrate, fireplace system, condition and required clearances must be assessed first. Decorative plaster is not a substitute for a fire-rated assembly or a repair to the appliance. Explore our fireplace transformation service for the decisions involved.

Preparing for a Surrey Project Consultation

Send a full-wall photo, approximate dimensions, your neighbourhood and the finish you have in mind. Include existing paint damage, visible cracks, outlets and any fireplace or cabinetry that meets the wall. For a tall stair or entry wall, ceiling height and access are useful details.

If you live in a condo or strata property, confirm any work-hour, elevator or common-area protection requirements before scheduling. For an occupied home, let us know how the room is used so we can discuss access and protection around the work.

To see how preparation affects a finished surface, view our nearby Brookswood, Langley brick-to-Marmorino fireplace project, with before-and-after photographs.

What to Include in Your Brief

  • A reference image showing the colour and texture you prefer
  • Whether the wall is part of a renovation or an occupied room
  • Any trim, cabinetry or electrical work planned around the surface
  • Your intended timing and the budget you are working toward
