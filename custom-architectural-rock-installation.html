Project Snapshot

Built as a custom focal surface

This custom sculptural surface combines layered relief, organic texture, moss integration, and ambient lighting in a finished interior feature.

The surface was designed to read as part of the room rather than a flat decorative treatment, with texture and light shaping how the feature changes from different angles.

Design Intent

Texture, shadow, and atmosphere

This project was designed around physical depth rather than a flat decorative finish. Irregular ridges, recesses, and natural-looking transitions create a surface that changes as light moves across the relief.

Integrated moss softens selected edges while ambient lighting emphasizes the depth and shadow of the sculpted surface.

Sculpting Process

Shaped by hand

The finished feature was shaped by hand to create irregular relief and rock-like texture rather than a repeating panel pattern.

For future projects, the process begins with a wall review, intended atmosphere, dimensions, lighting direction, and suitability check before a sculptural surface direction is recommended.

Craft Context

Custom Architectural Surfaces

Some interiors call for more physical depth than a conventional plaster or painted feature wall.

A sculptural surface can introduce relief, shadow, and organic geometry into a space while being designed specifically around the wall and surrounding interior. Every project should be reviewed individually before a finish direction is confirmed.
