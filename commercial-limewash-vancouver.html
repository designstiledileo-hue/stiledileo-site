Applications

Material character for commercial interiors.

Appropriate uses can include selected boutique and retail walls, showrooms, reception areas, client-facing workplaces and hospitality interiors.

The purpose is not to introduce texture everywhere. A carefully chosen decorative surface can sit quietly behind display systems, millwork, lighting and adjacent architectural materials.

Surface character

More than conventional paint.

Hand-applied finishes can produce subtle tonal movement and layered visual depth. The desired level of contrast, movement and texture should be established with reference samples appropriate to the actual project.

Large surfaces, grazing light and changing viewing angles make consistency and transitions especially important.

Project coordination

Readiness matters.

Before commercial finish application, substrate condition, prep responsibility, edges, access, adjacent finishes, lighting conditions and trade sequencing should be established. Stile di Leo's scope is the agreed specialty finish; other preparation is included only when explicitly arranged.

For project-specific work, samples can help confirm colour and the amount of visible movement before full application.

Alternative material

Limewash or Marmorino?

Different decorative finish systems bring different surface characteristics. If a more plaster-led texture or sheen is needed, Marmorino for retail interiors may be worth considering. The correct choice follows the surface requirements and design intent rather than a keyword.
